# Visualize and explore  your research data

OpenAtlas Discovery transforms complex research data into an **accessible, visual, and configurable presentation platform**. Explore your data through interactive maps, networks, and tables, while customizing the look and content of your website through an integrated CMS.

Try it out yourself:
::

## What Features does it offer?

We aim to make it as **accessible** as possible: Through our designed frontend, your research data is presented in a way that's easily navigable for end-users, providing a guided navigation experience through your data. The presentation platform is also aimed at domain experts, making your research even more visible.

It's **visual**: OpenAtlas Discovery aims to visualize your data using a map and network visualization.

And it's **configurable**: You can customize the page itself using the integrated **Content Management System** (in short CMS). This allows you to add your project logos, change colors, configure the map and network visualizations, create an individual team page, add new subpages, and all of this is quite user-friendly. This ease of use was very important to us in developing OpenAtlas Discovery to make sure that even non-techy researchers are not afraid of working with the cms and configuring their presentation site themselves.

---

## How does it work?

OpenAtlas Discovery consists of two parts: Firstly, the **pages that showcase your research data** provided and designed by us and secondly, the **pages that are customizable using an integrated Content Management System (CMS)**, such as the page where you introduce your project team or even the landing page.

::software-feature-small{image="/assets/images/hero-discovery-2.png" reverse ascpect-video}
### Part 1: Visualize your Instance

The first part of OpenAtlas Discovery presents your research data through core visualizations. These pages are designed and developed by us to provide **a clear, structured, and interactive display of your data**. Depending on the project, this can include visualizations such as maps, networks, and data tables, allowing users to explore the research material in different ways.
::



---



::software-feature-small{image="/assets/images/hero-discovery-2.png" ascpect-video}
### Part 2: Customize your Instance

The second part is the customizable website, managed through an integrated Content Management System (CMS).

The CMS allows you to edit and manage parts of the website without changing the underlying code. OpenAtlas Discovery connects the CMS with [GitHub](https://github.com/), so you can manage your website content using a GitHub account.

Through the CMS, you can **customize the website's visual appearance** and content, including logos, colors, landing pages, team pages, and additional subpages. You can also **configure aspects of the visualizations themselves**, allowing the presentation of your research data to be adapted to the needs of your project.
::

## Which Visualizations are available in OpenAtlas Discovery?

OpenAtlas Discovery offers three core visualization types: the **map**, the **network**, and the **data table**.

Each visualization provides a different way of exploring and understanding the research data, from geographical locations and movements to relationships between entities and individual data entries..

  ### The Map

  **Movement** can be visualized using animated arcs that represent direction, for example for travel routes. When multiple movements overlap, researchers can decide via the CMS whether these should be highlighted by thickness, helping to emphasize areas of dense activity. Colors for specific movement types can also be adjusted individually.

  Additionally, **icons** can be used for specific entities to add context and recognizability to the map.

  The map also supports the display of **certain and uncertain areas**, allowing precise areas to be shown alongside approximate ranges using lighter, sketched outlines. For example, to show graves as precise areas and the unknown location of a church as an uncertain one.
  :::

  :::feature-card
  ---
  tags:

  image: /assets/images/network.png
  ---
  ### The Network

  This view highlights connections between entities, making relationships within the dataset immediately visible. **Connections** are automatically visualized, helping users understand how different entities relate to one another and making navigation and exploration more intuitive.

  The view can be customized through the CMS to match the needs of each project. Researchers can configure **colors, entity types, and specific subsets of data**, allowing the network to focus on the relationships that matter most for a particular research question.

  This makes the network view especially useful for exploring complex datasets where **relationships and patterns** may be difficult to identify from individual entries alone.
  :::

  :::feature-card
  ---
  tags:

  image: /assets/images/Frame 20.png

  ### The Data Table

  This view provides a clear **overview** and easy **access to individual entries**, making it a useful starting point for exploring larger datasets. Researchers and end-users can quickly browse through the available information without needing to navigate a more complex visualization first.

  The table allows users to **sort and filter** research data, making it easier to find specific entries, compare information, and narrow down larger collections. This provides a straightforward way to move from an overview of the dataset to individual records.

  By combining accessibility with practical data exploration, the table helps users orient themselves within the research material before moving on to more detailed analysis or visualizations.
  :::
